Phrasa requires no account for review. Launch, skip onboarding, open the sample script, then open Reader. For Voice mode, grant Microphone and Speech Recognition, tap Play, and read several sentences aloud; the text should advance. On-device speech recognition is required when supported; otherwise Apple Speech may use Apple services. Speed mode works without permissions. Optional Sign in with Apple (primary) or email enables sync. After sign-in, scripts, folders, tags, and outlines sync automatically on launch, auth restore, foreground, and shortly after saved changes; Sync now is also available. Unsaved drafts are not uploaded. Reader settings remain local and are not uploaded. Account deletion is available in-app. Apple-linked deletion asks for a second Apple confirmation and does not claim Apple token revocation is complete until the server confirms it. There are no purchases or subscriptions. Privacy: https://phrasa.app/privacy Support: https://phrasa.app/support
